ABOUT US

We are CryptiCICS — a team of six from the College of Information and Computing Sciences, focused on learning HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. Our name combines "cryptic" and "CICS," reflecting how we think in codes and algorithms. Our logo is a digital lock, symbolizing security, mystery, and access through knowledge. Inside the lock are the initials of our surnames: Rodelas, Mira, Gonzales, Privado, Linga, and Macutong. These initials form the shape of the lock, showing how each of us plays a key role in the team. We aim to decode, defend, and dominate. In CryptoKnights, we’re not just competitors — we’re the architects of logic, protecting what matters in the digital world. We are CryptiCICS — and the key lies within us.
